date: 2026-08-10
summary: "Our first community call is on September 2nd. Come help shape the future of OAuth2 Proxy."

For the first time in OAuth2 Proxy's history, we are starting with monthly community calls. Our first call will take place on **September 2, 2026, from 13:00–14:00 UTC**.

We hope this will provide a space to connect with us the maintainers and fellow users, hear a preview of where the project is heading, and bring the topics that matter to you. 

If you are interested in security-focused open source tooling. Or open source in general, we would love to have you involved. In particular, there are opportunities to help improve documentation and integration guidance, triage and categorize issues and pull requests, and strengthen end-to-end testing.

[Join the community call](https://meet.google.com/dqw-zngc-xir) and bring your questions, ideas, and experience. Whether you are a long-time contributor or are just getting started with OAuth2 Proxy, your perspective can help shape the project.
