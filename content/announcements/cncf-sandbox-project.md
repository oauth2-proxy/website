It's official: OAuth2 Proxy is joining the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) as a Sandbox project! 🎉🥳

![OAuth2 Proxy joins CNCF as a Sandbox project](/images/cncf-sandbox.jpg)

We are now in the onboarding process and will be making changes to strengthen the project's governance and security compliance.

Inspired by the impressive efforts of the External Secrets maintainers, Gustavo Carvalho and Gergely Brautigam, over the last couple of weeks, we are also planning to introduce a contributor ladder with specialty tracks for OAuth2 Proxy. We hope this will encourage more people to get involved in testing, CI, reviewing provider and core changes, and helping oversee the project in the future.

We'll keep you posted on the progress and upcoming changes.
